Craterium pixel icon guidelines
Version 2.0, October 2026

Every icon in Craterium is pixel art on the same grid as the logo.
This file tells you exactly how to make a new one so it matches the set.
If a rule here and your taste disagree, the rule wins. Consistency is the
whole point of an icon set.


1. The grid
-----------

  Canvas:        12 x 12 cells. Always. No 16x16, no 10x10.
  Live area:     the inner 10 x 10 cells (columns 2 to 11, rows 2 to 11,
                 counting from 1). The outer ring of cells is padding.
  One cell:      one unit. A cell is either empty, foreground or accent.
                 There are no half cells and no partially filled cells.

  Allowed render sizes (integer multiples of 12 only):

      12 px  (1 screen pixel per cell)   dense tables, inline with 12 px text
      24 px  (2 per cell)                default for buttons, nav, toolbars
      36 px  (3 per cell)                empty states, settings headers
      48 px  (4 per cell)                onboarding, marketing
      96 px  (8 per cell)                hero, store listings

  Never render at 16, 20, 30 or 32 px. The browser has to resample a
  12 cell grid onto a size that does not divide by 12, and the edges go
  soft. That is the single most common way to break the pixel look.

  A shape may reach into the padding on one side only, and only when it
  needs the reach (an arrowhead, a tall stacked object). Never on two
  opposite sides at once. The checker flags it.


2. Cell types, mono and duotone
-------------------------------

  .   empty
  #   foreground. Always currentColor.
  +   accent. The one part that carries meaning: a cursor, an LED, an
      arrow, a keyhole, an active state. Maximum 12 accent cells.

  Every source produces two variants:

      duotone   # is currentColor, + is var(--crt-accent)
      mono      # and + are both currentColor

  So the mono variant is the duotone one with the accent folded in. Draw
  the icon so it still reads in mono. The accent must never be the only
  thing that makes the icon recognisable.

  Every icon has at least one accent cell, otherwise duotone equals mono.
  The checker flags icons without one.

  Never bake status colors (running, error and so on) into an icon.
  To show an error icon in red, set color: var(--crt-status-error) on the
  parent element. The icon follows. Never use more than two colors.


3. Strokes
----------

  Outline icons:   strokes are exactly 1 cell wide.
  Solid icons:     allowed for playback style actions only (play, stop,
                   pause). They are filled shapes, not outlines.
  2 cell strokes:  only for small symbols that must survive at 12 px:
                   plus, minus, check, close, chevrons, arrows, terminal,
                   the exclamation mark in alert.

  Do not mix 1 cell and 2 cell strokes inside one shape.


4. Straight lines and diagonals
-------------------------------

  Horizontal and vertical lines are unbroken runs of cells.

  Diagonals use one fixed step pattern from start to end:

      1:1 step (45 degrees)        2:1 step (shallow)

      #.....                       ##......
      .#....                       ..##....
      ..#...                       ....##..
      ...#..                       ......##

  Never mix step lengths in one straight line (1, 2, 1, 3 looks broken).
  A 1:1 diagonal in an outline icon is 1 cell wide, cells touch only at
  their corners. A 2 cell wide 1:1 diagonal is allowed for check marks,
  chevrons and arrowheads.


5. Curves and circles
---------------------

  Do not freehand circles. Use these recipes so every round shape in the
  set has the same rhythm.

  Large circle, 10 cells wide, 1 cell stroke (info, clock, radio):

      ....####....
      ..##....##..
      .#........#.
      .#........#.
      .#........#.
      .#........#.
      .#........#.
      .#........#.
      ..##....##..
      ....####....

  Medium circle, 8 cells wide, 1 cell stroke (search, uptime):

      ...####.....
      ..#....#....
      .#......#...
      .#......#...
      .#......#...
      .#......#...
      ..#....#....
      ...####.....

  Small circle, 6 cells wide, 1 cell stroke (user head, git-commit):

      ....####....
      ...#....#...
      ...#....#...
      ...#....#...
      ....####....

  Rounded rectangle corner: remove exactly one corner cell. Never more,
  the shape starts to look like a blob.

      ##########         .########.
      #........#   to    #........#

  The logo itself uses a 2 cell stroke circle. That weight is reserved for
  the logo and the API mark. Icons stay at 1 cell.


6. Gaps, holes and dots
-----------------------

  Minimum gap between two separate parts: 1 empty cell.
  Minimum hole inside a shape: 1 cell, better 2.
  Dots (LEDs, the dot of an exclamation mark, a cursor): at least 2 cells
  (2 x 1 or 2 x 2). A single cell with no filled neighbour is an orphan
  pixel. Orphans flicker visually at 24 px and disappear at 12 px.
  The checker flags them.


7. Symmetry and centering
-------------------------

  Symmetric objects must mirror exactly, cell for cell.
  The grid center sits between columns 6 and 7 (and rows 6 and 7).
  Even width shapes center perfectly. Odd width shapes shift half a cell
  left (and up), consistently.
  Shapes pointing right (play, chevron-right) sit one cell right of
  center, because their visual weight is on the flat side.
  Direction families (arrow-*, chevron-*) are drawn once and rotated or
  mirrored, never redrawn per direction.


8. Weight
---------

  Count the filled cells. Icons in the set fall in these ranges:

      outline icons    28 to 64 cells
      solid icons      30 to 64 cells
      small symbols    16 to 40 cells

  If your icon is far outside the range it will look too light or too
  heavy next to the others. Simplify or open up the shape.
  icons.json lists the cell and accent count of every icon.


9. Never
--------

  anti-aliasing, soft edges, half cells
  gradients, glows, drop shadows, outlines around the icon
  rotation by anything other than 90 degrees
  scaling to non multiples of 12
  smooth vector icons (Tabler, Lucide, Heroicons) in the same interface
  text inside icons
  more than two colors


10. Step by step
----------------

  1.  make new CAT=<category> NAME=<name>
      Categories: panel (server operations), ui (general interface),
      dev (files and development). Name: lowercase, kebab-case, named
      after the object, not the context. "folder", not "open-server-files".
      Names are unique across all categories.

  2.  Open src/<category>/<name>.txt in a monospace editor. Use replace
      mode (R in vim) so you overwrite cells instead of inserting.
      Every row must stay exactly 12 characters. Fill in the tags line.

  3.  Block out the silhouette with # inside the live area. Look for the
      simplest shape that is still recognisable. Start from the closest
      existing icon if one exists.

  4.  Apply the recipes from sections 4 and 5 to every diagonal and curve.

  5.  Mark the meaningful part with + (section 2).

  6.  make check
      It errors on wrong size, unknown characters or duplicate names, and
      warns on orphan pixels, missing or too many accent cells and shapes
      that reach the padding on two sides. Fix every warning.

  7.  make
      Writes svg/, png/, the sprites, icons.json and preview.html.

  8.  Open preview.html. Toggle mono and duotone, both themes and all
      sizes. Same weight, same rhythm, same padding as its neighbours?

  9.  Commit the .txt together with the generated files. The .txt is the
      source of truth. Never edit an SVG or PNG by hand.


11. Using icons in code
-----------------------

  Inline the SVG so currentColor works:

      <span class="crt-icon" aria-hidden="true">  <!-- svg here --> </span>

      .crt-icon svg {
        width: var(--crt-icon-size, 24px);   /* 12, 24, 36, 48 or 96 only */
        height: var(--crt-icon-size, 24px);
        display: block;
      }

  Or use a sprite:

      <svg width="24" height="24"><use href="sprite-duotone.svg#crt-server"/></svg>

  The SVGs carry shape-rendering="crispEdges", keep it.
  Icon only buttons need an aria-label on the button.
  If you use a PNG, scale it with nearest neighbour and set
  image-rendering: pixelated in CSS.


12. Checklist before you commit
-------------------------------

  [ ] 12 x 12, only . # +
  [ ] shape stays inside the live area (or one allowed exception)
  [ ] 1 cell strokes (2 only for the small symbols in section 3)
  [ ] diagonals use one step pattern
  [ ] circles use the recipes
  [ ] no orphan pixels, dots are 2+ cells
  [ ] 1 to 12 accent cells, accent only where it means something
  [ ] still recognisable in mono
  [ ] symmetric shapes mirror exactly
  [ ] weight within the ranges in section 8
  [ ] readable at 12 px
  [ ] make check passes, make has been run, everything committed
